# PulseFit 2.5.0

Released 2026-11-21. The Android app is 1.2.0 (versionCode 3).

## For members

- Forgot your password? If the gym has email set up, the reset link now arrives by email.
- When you book a class you get a confirmation email with the class, date and time.
- When you pay for a plan you get a receipt email with the plan, amount and reference.
- Gifts in the store now have pictures (the free week still shows its icon).
- In the Android app, each page has its cartoon inside the page, next to its heading or in a card, instead of a strip at the top. The website looks the same.
- The footer, the app menu and Visit us show Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and X icons that open each network's home page, until the gym adds its own pages.
- Android app: version 1.2.0. If you have 1.1.0 installed, it offers "Update available" once the new app file is uploaded.

## For staff and admins

- Emails need the Brevo settings (see `deploy/README.md`). Without them nothing is sent and the reset message tells people to ask the desk, as before. A slow or failing email never blocks a booking, payment or reset.
- Gym contact details: the four social links start as each network's home page. Enter your own page to replace one; clear it to hide that icon.
- Gifts: an admin's own gift emoji or upload is never replaced by the new pictures. New pictures are added with `npm run gift:add` (see `docs/gift-pictures/prompts.md`).
